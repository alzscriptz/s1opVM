Pair your devices

Open the s1op companion on your iPhone and choose Pair. Make sure both devices are connected to your trusted LocalDevVPN network. Follow the companion's prompts; don't share pairing codes with anyone.



Apple ID & signing

The installer may ask you to sign in with your Apple ID for app signing. Sign in only in the official Apple prompt or trusted installation app. This site never asks for your password, recovery key, or verification code.

Keep your account safeDon't type Apple ID credentials into this webpage or send them to anyone.

Install s1op

Use the open-source installer release and follow the on-device instructions to complete installation. The browser wizard doesn't install or sign an IPA by itself.
